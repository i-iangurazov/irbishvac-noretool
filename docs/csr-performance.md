# CSR performance

Routes: `/call-center/summary` and `/call-center/by-csr`.

Both views share one roster and the same exact Los Angeles business date range. The API will not substitute a YTD snapshot for MTD or a different custom period. Desktop/TV pages show four CSR cards; narrow screens use two or one columns. TV pages rotate every 15 seconds. Normal browser pages stay on the selected page.

## Metric definitions

- **Lead calls:** ServiceTitan CSR Booking Performance (`ST_REPORT_CALL_CENTER_PERFORMANCE`), `LeadCalls`.
- **Text leads:** the consolidated `Master Sheet` only, with `Call/Text` = Text and `Opportunity` = Good or Mid, following the October 15 CSR meeting. Call, Form, blank, and unknown request types are excluded. The meeting notes call the field `Request Type`; the verified January–October source workbooks label it `Call/Text`. Attribution uses the receiving `CSR`, not `Booked By`. Source tabs duplicate Master Sheet records and are never added again. Date filtering is inclusive of the selected business dates. The observed `Nina Naem` spelling is explicitly mapped to `Nina Naeem`; other names require normalized full-name matches.
- **Leads received:** lead calls plus qualified text leads. Unassigned or unmatched text leads are excluded from CSR totals and disclosed separately.
- **Booking rate:** inbound calls booked divided by lead calls. Team rate is calculated from total numerator and denominator, including employees with zero conversions. It is not an average of employee percentages, and does not mix text/manual bookings into phone conversion.
- **Booked jobs:** unique job numbers from Job Detail By CSR (report 177, configurable with `ST_REPORT_CSR_JOBS`), filtered by Job Creation Date (`DateType=2`) and attributed to `BookedBy`. Includes all booking channels. This replaces the old inbound-plus-manual count, which omitted some created jobs.
- **Sold rate:** unique jobs in that same booking cohort with at least one currently Sold estimate of positive subtotal, divided by all booked jobs for the CSR. Uses the existing ServiceTitan Sold Estimates report (`ST_REPORT_CAMPAIGN_SOLD_ESTIMATES`), matching `ParentJobNumber` to the booking report's `JobNumber`. Attribution stays with `BookedBy`, regardless of who sold the estimate. Multiple estimates on one job count once; unlinked/non-job estimates, open/dismissed estimates, and zero-value estimates do not count. Sold On is queried from the booking period's start through the latest refresh date, so September bookings that sell in October update September's rate. This is an estimate-sale metric, not invoice conversion or revenue. Historical cohorts refresh on demand when the supplementary cache is older than 10 minutes. Pending/cancelled bookings remain in the denominator. Zero bookings or unavailable sources display unavailable, not 0%; the team percentage uses total sold jobs divided by total booked jobs.
- **Cancelled jobs / cancellation rate:** the above created jobs whose current status is Canceled; cancellations divided by those unique booked jobs. Includes cancellations before and after dispatch.
- **Memberships sold:** Membership Sales Detail (report 921, `ST_REPORT_CSR_MEMBERSHIPS`), filtered by Sold On, attributed to `SoldBy`. These are membership sales, not membership renewals or the current membership balance.
- **Missed calls:** external inbound user call legs with the explicit `missed` category in Dialpad Analytics. Deduplicated by `call_id`; department/office entry-point rows, internal calls, spam and supervisor-monitoring legs are excluded. A subsequent callback or answer by another operator does not erase the original miss. Cards join the Dialpad user ID to a unique normalized full name, including the verified Adele Aldyrakhman → Adele Aldyrakhmanova alias. A known user with no misses shows 0; an unmatched user or unavailable source shows —. Summary sums matched CSR attempts and displays its coverage (for example, 7/8 CSRs) when anyone is unmatched. It is not a count of unique callers or of calls the whole department lost. ServiceTitan Abandoned is never substituted.

Dialpad’s missed outcome does not establish whether a CSR was on duty, on a break, or available. The current lines are Departments, while Dialpad's historical agent-status reports are for Contact Centers. Do not treat this metric as a verified availability or fault determination. A transfer to Hatch does not prove that Hatch answered; that requires a separate Hatch outcome source.

The roster includes CSR/customer-service roles, Membership Manager, Adele as CSR manager, and other office report employees with verified text-lead assignments. It excludes the synthetic Abandoned row and unrelated office staff. The underlying report excludes inactive employees; the summary is the displayed CSR team, not all company employees.

## Automatic delivery

The worker refreshes MTD every 30 minutes and YTD hourly. Supplementary reports are shared between both pages and cached for ten minutes per exact date range. Only aggregate supplementary data is persisted. Complete report pagination is required; rate-limited pages are retried with bounded delays. A failed source produces unavailable values rather than a fabricated zero.

`DIALPAD_API_KEY` is stored on the worker only. The integration reads the paginated user directory and generates call-log CSV exports via `POST /api/v2/stats`, then polls `GET /api/v2/stats/{id}`. Report generation does not email anyone. Historical days and today are requested separately in America/Los_Angeles, per Dialpad guidance. Today's analytics refresh about every 30 minutes; historical exports can lag 4–5 hours. The tooltip shows retrieval time, not an assertion of real-time completeness. Downloads validate HTTPS hosts on every redirect and never receive the API credential. Call rows and signed download URLs are not persisted or logged. The dedicated key created October 15, 2026 expires October 15, 2027; rotate it before expiry. Dialpad's mandatory Default scopes are broader than read-only access, so the credential must remain server-side.

`GOOGLE_CSR_REPORTS_FOLDER_ID` points to the shared CSR Reports folder. The existing Google service account needs Viewer access and Google Drive API enabled. Discovery reads spreadsheets directly in this folder and in its year/quarter subfolders. Monthly titles supply the reporting month; quarterly summaries and drafts are excluded. Explicit `GOOGLE_CALL_CENTER_SPREADSHEET_IDS_BY_MONTH` mappings take precedence. Duplicate monthly candidates are not guessed. Existing configured sheet IDs remain a fallback if Drive is unavailable.

Metadata is read before bounded Master Sheet ranges. January 2026 contains values appended to several header labels; its known label prefixes are supported without ingesting the header as a lead. Full requested-month coverage is required for text totals; a partial YTD set is never displayed as full-year data. The supplementary cache records the Text-only definition so earlier Text/Form aggregates are refreshed.

Historical Dialpad exports are split into non-overlapping windows of at most 31 inclusive business dates, with at most two reports loading concurrently. This bounds export size for YTD; it is an integration choice, not a Dialpad API range limit. Every requested window must succeed before any missed-call aggregate is published. Today's export remains separate. Interrupted GET requests are retried twice against the same report/download; report-creation POSTs are not retried. Historical connected legs without category lists are excluded as answered; unconnected legs without categories still fail validation instead of becoming missed calls or zeros.

Existing asset URLs supply employee photos, with initials when an image is missing. The shared header, logo, global navigation, Marketing dashboard and Campaigns integration key permissions are unchanged.
